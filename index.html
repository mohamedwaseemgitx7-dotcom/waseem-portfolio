Project details

MedForms Pro

Clinical Perfusion & ICU Forms Management · Live 2026

  • Full-stack web app that digitizes ICU and perfusion forms, replacing paper records with structured, searchable data.
  • Next.js and JavaScript for the interface with server-rendered pages and routing.
  • Backend services in Java (Spring Boot) alongside Python, integrated with Supabase for secure per-doctor data storage and authentication.
  • Exported data syncs to Google Sheets and Drive for ongoing reporting.
